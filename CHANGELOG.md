# What's new in Bon Temps

A plain-English list of updates, newest first. For the technical details, see the project's
[page changes](https://github.com/brody-robicheaux/bon-temps/commits/main/docs/index.html) and
[collector changes](https://github.com/brody-robicheaux/bon-temps/commits/main/collector/collect.py).

## Nov 21, 2026: Top picks photo row, more sources, faster approvals

- The site opens on **Top picks**, now with a swipeable row of photo cards. Events without a photo get a small Bon Temps poster with their name.
- Back on: the **Cajundome** (concerts, markets, championships), **Rock'n'Bowl** (Lafayette shows) and the **Lafayette Public Library** (special events only, like concerts and films; no storytimes or classes).
- Approved submissions show up within about 15 minutes instead of up to 2 hours.
- The submit form can take a photo or flyer link, and its category starts on "Not sure" so Bon Temps picks one (no more gumbo cook-offs filed under Music).
- Sharing a day's list previews as, for example, "Saturday, Nov 21 in Lafayette · 42 events".
- Easier to read and use: stronger contrast on tags and finished events, bigger buttons, clearer keyboard focus, better screen-reader headings.
- Behind the scenes: an email alert if a source stops working for a day.

## Nov 20, 2026: Event previews and Apple Calendar on the Home Screen

- Sharing an event now sends a link whose preview shows the event: name, day, time and venue.
- Apple Calendar now works from the Home Screen app too (it used to open a blank page).

## Nov 19, 2026: New link preview

- Texting or posting the Bon Temps link now shows a festival-poster card with the Acadiana flag: "Everything going on in Lafayette."

## Nov 18, 2026: Sports, bigger events and easier sharing

- New **Sports** filter with Ragin' Cajuns home games at the Cajundome and Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium.
- New **Civic** filter for town halls, public forums and community meetings, plus big events from the city government's calendar.
- Festivals, parades and holiday concerts are now added weeks ahead, from Lafayette Travel's full calendar. Only big events are taken from it, and the venue's own listing always wins.
- Noël Acadien au Village is listed every night, Dec 1–23.
- A yearly check flags big events (Festival International, Festivals Acadiens, Mardi Gras parades, Noël Acadien, Downtown Alive, Bach Lunch) that are coming up but not on the site yet.
- Search looks through every upcoming event, not just the day you're on, and finds "noel" without the accent.
- Today's events stay listed until midnight, dimmed once they're over.
- Apple Calendar button now works on iPhone and adds a reminder an hour before.
- Full event descriptions, and real venue names (like Cavalier House Books) instead of "Downtown Lafayette" or a bare address. Addresses are shorter.
- Swipe left or right on the list to change days.
- **Share this list** sends a link that opens on the same day and filters, like Saturday's music.
- Texting the Bon Temps link shows a preview card.
- "Something wrong with this listing?" on every event sends a correction to the submissions sheet.
- New heading font, and private visitor counts (GoatCounter).
- Duplicate and clutter checks run on every update.

## Nov 17, 2026: Theater, Top picks and submitting events

- **Submit an event** right from the site; every submission is reviewed before it goes live.
- New **Theater** filter, with shows from Cité des Arts, Wonderland, the Acadiana Center for the Arts and UL.
- Date strip: tap any day of the next two weeks. Holidays are marked.
- **Top picks**: the 10 best events of the coming week (replaced "Upcoming").
- All-day events sit at the bottom of each day.
- Real start times and venue names for many more events; clutter and low-quality listings filtered out.
- Venue pages: see everything coming up at one place.
- Saved events stay on your phone, and a tip shows how to add Bon Temps to the Home Screen.
- The app updates itself when a new version comes out.

## Nov 16, 2026: Bon Temps launches

- Live music, food, arts, wellness and family events around Lafayette, gathered automatically from venue calendars and local listings every couple of hours.
- Blue Moon Saloon, the Heymann Center, Moncus Park, Downtown Lafayette, the Hilliard and more.
- Night mode, saved events, and Google or Apple Calendar for any event.
